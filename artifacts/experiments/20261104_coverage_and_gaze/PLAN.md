# Coverage and gaze qualification — 2026-11-04

Read-only model audit requested by the user. No world training, sensor change,
probe fitting, or GPU intervention. This follows the completed diagnostic
boundary review; it does not reopen the frozen component-search ladder.

1. Audit the original F1.5 gaze tests and their already-completed F2 equivalents.
   Distinguish physical current-query invariance from acquired-evidence uptake.
   A useful crop must add information beyond coarse vision and prior history;
   reader disagreement alone does not establish this prerequisite.
2. Count all unique events in the actual P1 training split, excluding the fixed
   monitor episodes. Report attempts separately from successful increments and
   once-per-episode achievements. Count damage, low-health adjacent-zombie risk,
   and death, with episode and epsilon support. Check actual repeated F2 world
   exposure using its saved trace. Repeated epochs are not independent events.
3. Compare archived danger/achievement predictions under the SAME random and
   guided evaluation conditions across model versions. Preserve real-target
   rates, real-frame-head scores, sampling settings, and cohort sizes.
4. Explain what a fine-required native training task would change, which raw
   data exist, and which qualification/paired examples do not yet exist. This
   is a discussion proposal, not a ready or approved training experiment.

Counts can establish rarity and missing support, not that more samples would
cure a failure. Older model differences on the same corpus rule out a changed
P1 corpus as the sole explanation; they do not isolate architecture from loss,
acquisition policy, optimization, or finite-seed variability.
