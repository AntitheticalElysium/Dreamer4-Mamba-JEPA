Predeclared 2026-10-05 before research training. Code: `e19.py`; mechanics evidence:
`e19_verify_cpu.json`, `e19_verify_cuda.json`, `e19_batch40_smoke.json`.

## Matched treatments

| arm | input boundaries | loss |
|---|---|---|
| A | original six-frame window | historical teacher L1 |
| B | random split, score both retained segments | teacher L1, same 200 original targets/batch |
| C | exactly B's windows, actions and splits | B + mask-normalized health-token L1, lambda1 |

Both fmamba and full-attention controls, seeds7/8, initialize from their **own 36k
parent**. Fresh canonical phase AdamW, lr1e-4, wd0.01, warmup1000, clip1, bf16;
6,000 updates, 40 original windows/update. Main TRAIN holdout2048, seed1;
batch generator seed11, boundary generator seed19. Each arm consumes the boundary
draw, even when A ignores it. A/B/C therefore have identical sampled-row/offset
ledgers. No fork outcomes train these worlds. C's TRAIN-derived zombie-context mask
is a task-specific diagnostic objective, not claimed as an unsupervised paper recipe.

For offset r in0..4, start=4-r. Predict original transitions0..start-1 in the
original prefix and start..4 in the suffix. The boundary frame is input to both;
**every original target/action/mask is scored exactly once**. Split terminal AND
nonterminal windows with the same distribution. Normalize the summed teacher error
by the original batch*5*81*192, never an average of segment means. C's extra term
uses the original selected-mask count*192. Mask only selects loss terms; it is never
an inference input. Health is tile63, not72.

## What the contrast can and cannot identify

Parent→A measures additional updates/optimizer restart. B−A measures the specified
boundary/context intervention (some histories become shorter and recurrent states
reset). C−B measures extra health dose on **identical contexts**. It does not isolate
positional embeddings from history/reset changes, or prove the dose is the only cause.

Uniform terminal row counts do **not** imply uniform death prevalence per row.
Expected target counts per window after splitting are[1.8,1.4,1.0,0.6,0.2]. On the
exact TRAIN pool, expected death prevalence at predictor rows0..4 is approximately
[2.96%,3.80%,5.33%,8.88%,26.63%], versus[0,0,0,0,26.63%] in A. This broadens exposure
to deaths but retains a row-dependent prior; **B is not labelled alias-free**. The
position/repeated-frame evaluations directly test remaining dependence rather than
assuming the sampler eliminated it. All 8,149 deaths and 2,000 ordinary >=2 drops
remain training targets. C covers1,636/2,000 ordinary drops;364 remain outside it.

The older crop/right-pad scratch design is rejected: it loses10.5507% of targets
and18.1700% of ordinary damage examples. It is not the launched implementation.

## Primary-source rationale and differences

[IRIS dataset](https://github.com/eloialonso/iris/blob/main/src/dataset.py) samples
episode segments with random endpoints and padding; its
[world model](https://github.com/eloialonso/iris/blob/main/src/models/world_model.py)
masks padded labels. This supports checking terminal alignment, but IRIS's sampling
does not establish that our target-preserving split removes every positional prior.
[VaGraM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.01464) addresses the mismatch between dynamics
reconstruction and policy-relevant error; its value-gradient objective is **not** our
zombie-context mask. These sources motivate interventions, not causal conclusions.
