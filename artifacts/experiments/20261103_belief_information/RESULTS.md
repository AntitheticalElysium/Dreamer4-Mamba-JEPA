# Fine evidence, mixer, belief and native consumer: completed reading

2026-11-03. Frozen F1.6ck10; all121 source/input pins reverified with zero changes.
12 fixed-budget readers completed; two seeds,150 disjoint confirmation episodes /
51,923 target rows. Native paired panel:64 distinct episodes/scenes x49 genuine
looks, fixed future query/target/action, no scroll;33 queries repeat the original
look and31 are independent. World unchanged. No followup experiment launched.

## What is established

The fine observation improves a trained readout of the **fine target**, even
after future peripheral/light evidence and older fine history are supplied.
This is incremental practical prediction value; it does not establish unique
information-theoretic content or additional behavior-relevant facts.
Confirmation next-fine prediction error reductions versus the same baseline:

| Current supplement | Seed612101 | Seed612102 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Raw current glimpse |9.87% [9.13,10.47]|8.69% [8.02,9.29]|
| Local joint fusion |8.84% [8.13,9.43]|8.51% [7.87,9.06]|
| Actual global mixed input |4.05% [3.31,4.70]|3.87% [3.26,4.51]|
| Retained joint recovered from BF16 mixture |3.20% [2.76,3.60]|3.07% [2.60,3.48]|
| Native post-action belief |−0.56% [−0.93,−0.23]|−0.70% [−0.99,−0.42]|

Intervals are paired episode-bootstrap95% intervals, conditional on this single
frozen world, not variability across world-training seeds. These are readout
performance differences, not percentages of information literally destroyed.

This is not just an aggregate covariance effect: changing the actual current
look, while keeping future query/target and all prior history fixed, improves
raw-fine AND local-joint readers in both seeds after subtracting gaze-only base
contrasts. The local-joint advantage exceeds mixed-input advantage directly:
0.01823 [0.00752,0.02855] and0.02159 [0.01174,0.03163] standardized error units.
Thus the practical accessibility drop at mixing is repeatable on genuine looks.
After the belief update, no fine-specific look advantage is qualified; intervals
include zero in both seeds. The frozen native denoiser likewise has no measurable
fine benefit on this panel: mean fractional fine gain−0.0022%,95% interval
[−0.0221%,0.0163%], averaged over the eight declared noise levels/two draws.
This is denoising, not a fresh actor or free-running sampler experiment.

The effect is not uniform across content. HUD fine error reduces24.4–24.8% from
raw fine,19.6–21.1% from mixed, and4.2–4.6% from belief in aggregate readouts.
Map fine error reduces4.4–5.7% from raw fine; mixed does not help overall and
belief does not help. Do not claim every trace is erased. Genuine-look controls
are inconclusive separately at night and for pure map-query cohorts, so no
night-specific memory-erasure conclusion follows. Entity paired subset has only
12 scenes; it is a secondary regional read, not the broad diagnosis.

## The other target gives a different answer

For **next peripheral tokens**, raw fine adds only0.016–0.030%, with intervals
including zero in both seeds. Genuine-look raw-fine controls also fail. No missing
predictive benefit is demonstrated for that target, so downstream nulls there
cannot diagnose failed memory. The6.86–6.87% mixed-input and5.52–5.61% belief
aggregate gains on this target are not evidence of fine-specific use: their
supplements also re-express coarse/context information.

The user's redundancy hypothesis is therefore supported as a practical concern
for the peripheral target, but not as a general explanation of fine prediction.
A finite reader null is not a proof of statistical conditional independence.

## What remains unidentified; disposition

We have localized **usable-access deterioration**, not proven irreversible
projection loss, an inherently incapable Mamba, or a uniquely defective denoiser.
Finite trained readouts cannot distinguish all of compression, coding geometry,
insufficient learning pressure, and native dynamics. Some projected information
is recoverable; the global mixer and native belief are not interchangeable nulls.
No single-stage architectural repair is justified as a proven cure.

Stop the component-ablation ladder here and discuss. Require demonstrated
incremental fine value for the same target before interpreting any downstream
null. P1-F2 is the predeclared test of changed acquisition/teaching on unchanged
F1.6. Better world scores alone do not prove newly acquired fine evidence was
remembered; an unchanged score does not prove no useful fine evidence exists.
Its already configured genuine-look uptake reads and common-gaze evaluation
must accompany the result. The coarse-only chooser can select informative
training targets even where coarse input already contains the answer; it is not
an oracle for the coarse-versus-fine information gap.
