# Review of transformer and acuity revision, c1bfd2d0

**Disposition:** a transformer is a reasonable first backbone. Its compact token interface and future memory interfaces are good reasons to choose it. The previous convolutional proposal was also coherent with the belief-MDP contract; noise-packing requirements did not make it invalid. Backbone choice, diffusion-forcing/curriculum changes, and a degraded input sensor are three separable decisions.

**What I checked.** Read the attached revision, current design, committed EDA scripts and the transformer timing prototype; independently reran the pooled-separation and appearance-residual EDAs on their original dev data; checked temporal dependency reach; read the primary Dreamer 4, JEDI and RoPE sources. No model training or final-split evaluation. Package and design files are unchanged. The initial direct appearance-script invocation failed because the root package was not on its import path; rerunning with `PYTHONPATH=.` succeeded. Protocol: [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md).

**The HUD distance comparison mixes statistics.** At `eda_pooled_separation.py:29–32`, the map statistic is the median of all pairwise prototype distances. The HUD statistic is the median, over positions, of each position's MINIMUM distance. Calling these simply map median 69 versus HUD median 1 overstates the comparison.

| 3x3 pooled prototypes | Matched descriptive statistic |
|---|---:|
| Map: median pairwise RMS | 69.0726 |
| HUD: median over positions of their median pairwise RMS | 29.2053 |
| HUD: median over positions of their minimum pairwise RMS | 1.00377 |

There are three exact colliding HUD pairs out of 321 within-position pairs in this sample. They occur at three of the eight analysed positions; most prototype pairs remain distinct. These are unweighted prototype counts, not class-frequency-weighted decoding accuracies. The script examines eight positions in HUD row 7, not the full two-row HUD. The map comparison covers 27 prototype labels under its full-light/awake restriction, not all 44 labels in the wider sample. Evidence: [pooled_statistics.json](pooled_statistics.json).

This supports some loss of fine distinctions. It does not establish that the periphery sees only an icon or that a fixed small noise makes its count generally inaccessible. Such noise also leaves temporal inference open: repeated observations, actions, rewards and potentially the viewing-conditions path can carry additional information. Any readability claim needs to state those inputs, rather than examining one local pooled cell only.

**The 0.003-bit result is a raw-image fact, not a learned-encoder result.** Reproduction confirms H(pixels | simulator label) about 0.003 bits over 20,979 full-light, awake cells. This establishes near-deterministic rendering for the selected labels and conditions. It does not measure H(label | learned periphery token), which is the quantity needed to say the periphery already knows a distinction. Nor does it show that the jointly learned tokens retain HUD details or that a larger fovea cannot improve practical extraction. The label set is not an exhaustive definition of task-relevant appearance: arrow direction is omitted, while the installed renderer explicitly changes arrow appearance by direction (`renderer.py:340–368`). Small average residual entropy is not grounds to discard rare consequential distinctions. Output: [appearance_residual.log](appearance_residual.log).

The challenge to the original capacity explanation is nevertheless valid: continuous width alone does not impose a dependable information-rate or acuity limit. Practical precision, noise, network capacity, optimization and objectives affect retention. “Can encode the information” must remain separate from “will encode it under this training recipe.” Neither daytime redundancy nor daytime benefit of the fovea has been demonstrated by the present EDA.

**A versus B defines different experimental conditions.** A asks what the learned periphery/fovea split achieves from the agreed full-resolution input. B explicitly imposes a lower-acuity observation channel. B is a legitimate foveated-sensing experiment, especially if such an observation/compute budget is the intended research problem. It is not the only logically possible setting with a useful fovea. A prior decision can be reconsidered, but the present results do not compel that reversal. My default recommendation remains A for M0, with B the previously planned explicit comparison. Avoid choosing noise solely until particular Craftax glyphs become hard to decode; choose a general sensor limitation and quantify its consequences if adopting B.

**A transformer does not force a curriculum change.** The proposed one-pass diffusion-forcing implementation is one possible transformer training procedure. JEDI reconstruction switching requires extra computation in that implementation, but is not incompatible with transformers. Gaussian-noised context and substitution of model reconstructions exercise different error distributions. JEDI's ablations call switching helpful rather than critical; that makes removing it a plausible declared simplification, not an equivalence result. Dreamer 4 supports independent per-frame noise levels and autoregressive generation with lightly noised context. These are relevant precedents, not validation of our added recurrent belief path.

Specify what the belief updater sees during P1. If Mamba consumes clean recorded percepts while only the transformer's history is noised, that does not train Mamba's correction on generated percept errors. The P2 free-running loop addresses that remaining gap; do not describe it as already solved by input corruption. Keep action/percept/reward timing causal and the predicted belief fixed during denoising.

**The efficient sequence form needs an explicit cache contract.** I inspected the cost-only `tf_cost.py` in the current Claude scratchpad. It contains six spatial blocks, three with temporal attention; each temporal mask permits the preceding four frames. Composing the masks yields a path from frame 51 to frame 63, a 12-frame maximum lookback before accounting for Mamba's history. This is possible information flow, not a learned-use measurement. Thus the full-sequence training form is not automatically the same four-raw-frame conditional model as the separately expanded window benchmark. State temporal positions, attention masks, episode resets, cached layer states and how inference reproduces training. Either declare the effective state/context or constrain it consistently. A small prefix-versus-streaming check is sufficient; no new architecture study is needed.

**Other qualifications.** Four projections of Mamba's output are four readout tokens, not four independently enlarged memories. RoPE plus absolute embeddings is a plausible position recipe, but the Heo result concerns resolution interpolation of models trained on larger images, not a trial training our 9x9 world model; make temporal ordering explicit too. Comparing our 25.6M training exposures to other models' environment-data counts confuses repeated training with unique experience: the stated corpus has 2.56M training transitions. The full-model throughput check remains the authority; 0.30 ms extra per target corresponds to about 2.13 GPU-hours per world seed, or 4.27 hours across both P1 seeds, before other changed costs.

Primary sources: [Dreamer 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.24527), [JEDI](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.13013v1), [RoPE for ViT](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.13298v2). Their claims should remain separate from the project adaptations above.

The useful next action is to lock one backbone and one explicit training/inference contract. The new EDA warrants a more careful account of the fovea's potential role; it does not establish that full-resolution M0 cannot test a useful fovea or that lowering input quality must be adopted now.
