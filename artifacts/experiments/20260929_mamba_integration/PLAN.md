# Patch-token Mamba integration: predeclared sequence (2026-09-29)

Question: Can a Raw+LDAD encoder, patch-token state, temporally trained Mamba-2, and corrg output support reliable imagined control? This is a declared LeWM variant, not the canonical CLS/z route. Do not infer actor success from pixel/token error or a fitted probe.

Inputs frozen before the first run: the canonical Raw H2 encoder and six-frame TRAIN-only pool (`spatial_pool_v1`), Raw+LDAD lambda-1 and lambda-10 joint step-010000 encoders, and the existing experimental `TWorld` `fmamba` and `corrg` implementation. Record SHA256 of every source/checkpoint/pool in each result. Preserve every negative state and report.

Subrun 0 (mechanics and state): Re-encode exactly the six-frame Raw pool IDs with the frozen lambda-1 encoder, copy every non-state field unchanged, assert byte-identical ID/action/label arrays and TRAIN-only membership, then compare root patch-token fact readouts with Raw and lambda-10 using identical fits and seeds. The existing z readouts are not substitutes for patch-token readouts. Stop on lineage mismatch.

Subrun 1 (short integration): Train three worlds with `--backbone fmamba --head corrg --loss suffix` on the same six-frame IDs, batches, seed 7, optimizer, and updates: Raw, lambda-1, lambda-10. Save 6k and 12k snapshots and the 18k endpoint. The first 6k is the matched short-batch architecture check; 18k is required because corrg's existing full-backbone route needed that budget. Report time/step, peak GPU RAM, one-step x-copy by action type, blocked/moved decisions, depth 1/4/8/16 recursion, and generated-fit semantic facts. Across different latent spaces, x-copy and /V are descriptive, not a quality ranking. If one arm fails, diagnose data, optimization, route, generated-state transfer, and source implementation before altering the recipe. Do not silently rerun with new hyperparameters.

Subrun 2 (length, after code/resource smoke): Extend the same per-tile fmamba model to sequence length 64, preserving the six-layer spatial-attention/temporal-Mamba architecture and corrg head. Build long windows from the same TRAIN corpus and identical `(episode_id,start)` ledger across encoder arms; keep episode/split boundaries, label alignment, and action alignment exact. Predeclare window count, terminal coverage, update count, effective transitions/update, unique windows/update, and the memory-admitted batch size after a resource smoke but before reading any treatment metrics. Continue the three Subrun-1 worlds from their same 18k snapshot. For lambda-1, also continue a six-frame-reset temporal control on the same long windows and targets; compare full-history and rolling-six evaluation on the same long-trained model. These two contrasts isolate temporal carry from training-data exposure more cleanly than a separately sampled short-window run. Measure full-history versus bounded-window predictions by position, Mamba state norm by position, revisit and zombie/night strata, and depth-16 recursion. The proposed 64-frame loss supervises 63 one-step teacher predictions and only a two-step generated suffix. Thus 48 observed + 16 imagined falls inside the total sequence length but is not a trained 16-step generated path. Any depth-16 measurement is an explicit out-of-distribution diagnostic, never evidence that H16 is trained. Before an H16 actor, train/evaluate a separately declared generated-depth bridge that exposes the model and outcome heads to the deployment depths without a depth/position alias. A length gain without a decision gain remains a memory finding, not actor evidence.

Subrun 3 (decision and actor): Freeze training/model choices before opening fresh all-action Craftax seeds 65000+. Fit diagnostic heads separately on real and generated successor states using only TRAIN/FIT roots. Judge within-root safe-action choice on the fresh block with paired episode/seed intervals; include DOWN, action-only, root+action, real-successor, and generated-successor controls, selected-action histogram, zombie/night/opportunity counts, and transfer calibration. Keep diagnostic fork labels out of the world and actor training. Train the same alias-free generated-state reward/continuation/actor procedure for each selected world and compare actual held-out game rollouts with matched BC. A go claim requires a resolved real-control advantage, not just token loss or AUC. A head failure triggers depth/position and real/generated transfer diagnostics; a world failure triggers per-action, per-token, exposure, and context-state diagnostics.

Literature checked: [Delta-JEPA](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.31232v1) supports action-difference supervision but no Craftax dose; [Stuffed Mamba](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07145) finds train/deployment length mismatch can collapse recurrent states; [Dreamer 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.24527v1) alternates short/long batches and explicitly trains batch lengths beyond its context to avoid start-frame overfitting; [Po et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.20171v1) find block-size-one temporal scans can underperform on spatial retrieval. These are motivations and failure hypotheses, not explanations for our measured results. Dreamer 4’s diffusion/shortcut-forcing objective and generated-context corruption differ materially from this latent L1 predictor; its sequence-length rule is not a substitute for testing our recursive generation.

The old deferred Direct-head second seed and full-Transformer 36k curve are not on this queue. Do not expand to fcanvas/fscan or change the loss unless the measured failure localizes to alignment, temporal mixing, or output route respectively.

Length-interface specification, fixed before any long-world result: use the same six learned time embeddings periodically at length 64 (`time[t % 6]`) in both continuation arms. It isolates cross-boundary recurrence, although its effective context averages less than a sliding six-frame window.
